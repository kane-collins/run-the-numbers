About

Irish and international running, by the numbers.

I'm Kane. I founded Run the Numbers to put my data science experience to work on the questions I find most interesting in Irish and international running: who's really consistent, what it takes to win, and what progression and development actually looks like.

Every piece starts with one of those questions. I follow the data wherever it goes, then show it through interactive charts you can explore yourself, and explain what it all means in plain English.
