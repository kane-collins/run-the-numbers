Three interactive charts on how a year of weather really adds up.

We talk about the weather constantly, but rarely look at it as a whole. Laid out month by month, a year of weather has a shape, and some surprises.

When does it actually rain?

Start with the basics: total rainfall by month. The wettest stretch isn't the middle of the year, it's the dark end of it.

Autumn and winter deliver more than half of the year's rain.

Two years, side by side

Averages hide a lot. Comparing two individual years shows how differently the same calendar can play out, with a dry spring in one and a washed-out summer in the other.

Warmer means sunnier?

Mostly, yes. Plot each month's average temperature against its hours of sunshine and the months line up in a loose diagonal.
